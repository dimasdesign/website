TEMPUS EDAX RERUM

"Devouring Time, and you, Jealous Age, consume everything, and slowly gnawing at them, with your teeth, little by little, consign all things to eternal death!"

"Tempus edax rerum, tuque invidioſa vetuſtas, Omnia deſtruitis: vitiataque dentibus ævi Paulatim lenta conſumitis omnia morte!"
